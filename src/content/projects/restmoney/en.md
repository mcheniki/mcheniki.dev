RestMoney is the tool I built to track my freelance income and see what remains once contributions
and taxes have been set aside throughout the year. It offers a useful day-to-day reference, without
replacing professional accounting advice.

It brings together an annual dashboard, income tracking, URSSAF rate settings and a guest demo
with fictional data.
