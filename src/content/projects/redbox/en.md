Redbox Media needed to streamline the delivery of its web projects while improving website performance and the development team's day-to-day workflow.

As Tech Lead, I designed a lightweight, modular WordPress starter theme powered by Vite and set up a CI/CD pipeline with Bitbucket Pipelines to make deployments more reliable. Depending on the project, I also worked with JavaScript, React/Next.js and PHP. I supported developers with problem-solving and architecture decisions.

The role combined hands-on development, technical ownership and coordination: working closely with the lead designer, improving team processes and creating a reusable foundation for the agency's projects.
